Support

How to play

Pick a game on the Games screen, add the players and tap Start. Every game explains itself the first time you open it, and the ? button shows the rules again. Pass the phone round when the screen says so: some steps are secret.

Restoring Plus

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings › Plus › Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling Plus and refunds

Turn off auto-renew in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions (Manage subscription in the app's Settings opens it), at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep Plus until the end of the period you have paid for. Apple handles refunds: ask at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Without Plus, a full-screen ad shows after every 2 finished games, never during a game. Ads are never personalised. Plus removes them.

I saw an inappropriate ad

Tap Settings › About › Report an ad (or email penaltyroom1@gmail.com) and say what the ad showed and roughly when. We use AdMob's controls to block it where we can.

Where are my players and scores?

On this phone only. They are not shared between phones, and deleting the app erases them. If you move to a new iPhone by restoring a backup, they come with it.

Something wrong?

Email penaltyroom1@gmail.com with what happened and the phone model, and we will sort it.
