Shenanigans

Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026

The short version

Shenanigans is a pass-the-phone party game made by Fadil Ahmed ("we", "us"). There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server: the names, scores, settings and words you enter stay on your phone, and we never receive any of it.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. They are never personalised and the app never tracks you, so it never asks Apple's "Allow tracking" question and shows no ad-permission pop-ups, in any country. Plus subscribers see no ads.

Plus is bought and paid for through Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

What stays on your phone

The names you type for players, your rule settings, scores, the words already used, the game in progress and any custom words you add are saved only on your device, so the app can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Player names are whatever you choose to type. They do not need to be real names.

The app has no analytics or tracking services of its own.

Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

Without Plus, a full-screen ad shows after every 2 finished games, as you leave the result screen; never during a game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, through Google's ad software built into the app (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). So that an ad is ready in time, the app asks Google for one in the background while the phone is online.

The ads work the same way in every country. Every ad request is marked as non-personalised, as child-directed and as coming from someone under the age of consent, for every player whatever their age, and is limited to ads rated G (general audiences, suitable for everyone). This tells Google to apply its strictest protections: no personalised ads and no remarketing.

Nothing is used to track you. The app never asks Apple's "Allow tracking" (App Tracking Transparency) question because it does not track: it never has your device's advertising identifier, information from the app is never combined with information from other companies' apps or websites to target or measure ads, and it is never shared with data brokers. There are no ad-permission pop-ups and no ad-personalisation settings, because there is nothing to personalise or opt into.

What Google receives, and why. To show ads, Google's ad software collects: your IP address, from which a rough location can be worked out (about city level, never your precise location); an identifier for your device that is not the advertising identifier; advertising data (which ads were requested, shown or tapped); basic information about how the app is used, such as launches and taps on ads; and crash, performance and other diagnostic data about its software, such as the phone model, system version, loading times and errors.

Google uses this only to deliver these non-personalised ads, limit how often you see the same ad, count and report ads in total, find and fix problems in its software, and prevent fraud and abuse. We never receive it, and none of it is linked to you by us: it carries no name, email or account from the app. How Google handles it: https://policies.google.com/privacy and, for apps that show its ads, https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Reporting an ad

If an ad seems wrong for the app or for younger players, tap Settings › About › Report an ad. It opens an email to us with space to say what the ad showed and roughly when you saw it. We read every report and use AdMob's controls to block that ad or advertiser where we can. You can also email us directly at penaltyroom1@gmail.com.

Plus and payments (Apple)

Plus is bought through Apple's App Store with your Apple Account, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Apple receives the purchase, as with any App Store purchase; we never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. On your phone, Apple's StoreKit tells the app the prices in your currency and whether this Apple Account has an active subscription. The answer is kept on your phone so the games still open without a signal.

Who receives information, and how it is protected

The app shares information with only two companies: Google, for the ads described above, and Apple, for the subscription. Each of them is bound by its own privacy policy and data protection terms to give your information the same or equal protection as this policy: Google at https://policies.google.com/privacy, Apple at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your phone's Analytics settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage totals; they do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Internet connection

The games work without a connection. When the phone is online, the free version loads ads and the app checks your subscription with Apple. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and while the phone is offline nothing is sent at all.

Children

The app is made for everyone; it is not made specifically for children. We collect no personal information ourselves, and every ad request is treated as child-directed and under the age of consent, so children can play without giving us anything, and the ads they see are never personalised and are limited to G-rated content. Parents can turn off in-app purchases in Screen Time on iPhone. If you think a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Everything the app saves is on your phone and stays there until you delete it. You can remove players, reset scores and clear custom words in the app; deleting the app removes all of it.

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you email us, we keep the email only to reply and delete it within 12 months of the matter being closed.

Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). To ask Google about it or have it deleted, use https://policies.google.com/privacy, or contact us and we will pass your request on. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. We hold none from the app itself. For information Google's ad software received, contact Google or contact us and we will pass your request on. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

If you live in California or another US state with privacy laws: we do not sell or share your personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and on our website, and the date above changes. Questions: penaltyroom1@gmail.com.
